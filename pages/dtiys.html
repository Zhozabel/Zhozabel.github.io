The challenge brought together artists with different styles and approaches to the same character.

The character

Matушка (Tsuki Shuuki)

Matушка is an original character first created in 2020. She is one of the ten members of the Shuuki clan, serving the cyclical beings.

She is a beautiful, slender woman with long white hair, red eyes and numerous golden ornaments. Her rich red layered clothes are decorated with symbols of the Lower Path.

Calm, reserved and self-confident, she can also be kind, gentle and caring towards her adopted daughters.

Winners

Five people evaluated the artworks: four invited judges and the challenge organizer.

Each judge assigned places from first to third. If an artwork did not make a judge's top three, it received no place. Such entries are represented by null and counted as zero when calculating the average score. The lower the average, the higher the final result.
