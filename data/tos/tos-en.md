- · I reserve the right to decline any commission without providing a reason.
- · Please provide clear references and a detailed description of your request. Any special requirements must be discussed

## 2. PRICING

- · All base commission prices are calculated per character.
- · Each additional character is charged separately according to the applicable base commission price.
- · Backgrounds are priced separately and charged **once per illustration**, regardless of the number of characters.

### Background pricing

- · **No background:** Free.
- · **Geometric background or solid-color fill:** Free.
- · **Simple illustrated background:** $5.
- · **Complex illustrated background:** $10.

Background complexity and pricing will be discussed and agreed upon before work begins. If the requested background
turns out to be significantly more complex than initially described, the price may be adjusted after discussion and with
your approval.

### Highly detailed characters

An additional fee may apply to characters with particularly intricate designs, such as numerous accessories, elaborate
patterns, complex clothing, jewelry, or other details that require significantly more time to draw.

Any additional fee will be discussed individually and approved by the client before work begins.

All prices, additional fees, and the final scope of the commission must be agreed upon in advance.

## 3. PAYMENT

- · I accept payments through **PayPal**, **Ko-fi**, **Hipolink** and **Boosty**. Bank transfers are also available for clients in
  Kazakhstan and Russia.
- · Full payment is due after the sketch has been approved and before I begin the line art.
- · I will not proceed to the line art stage until payment has been received.
- · The commission scope and total price must be agreed upon before work begins.
- · Any additional fees must be discussed and approved in advance.

- · Unpaid sketches are for review purposes only. They may not be used, published, redistributed, or presented as the

## 4. WORKFLOW AND APPROVAL

The usual commission process is:

1. We discuss the request, references, requirements, price, and estimated timeline.
2. I prepare the sketch.
3. The client reviews the sketch and requests any necessary changes.
4. Once the sketch is approved, the client makes the full payment.
5. I proceed with the line art, coloring, and final rendering.
6. I deliver the finished illustration in the agreed format.

Please review the sketch carefully before approving it. Approval means that I can proceed to the next stage of work.

Deadlines are discussed individually. If I expect a significant delay, I will inform the client.

## 5. REVISIONS

The commission includes **three free rounds of substantial revisions during the sketch stage**.

A substantial revision is a change that requires significant reworking of the composition, pose, character placement or
interaction, or another major visual element.

A revision round means one agreed set of changes established during our conversation. Multiple messages, clarifications,
and follow-up details relating to the same agreed set of changes count as one round, not several.

A new revision round applies when the client requests additional changes beyond the previously agreed set, including
changes requested after an earlier revised element has already been approved.

Additional revision rounds are charged as follows:

- · During the sketch stage: **5% of the original commission price per additional round**.
- · After the line art has begun: **10% of the original commission price per additional round**.
- · After coloring has begun: **15% of the original commission price per additional round**.

Minor adjustments, such as small color changes or minor details, are not automatically considered substantial revisions.

Corrections needed because my work does not match the description or requirements originally agreed upon will be made
free of charge.

Major changes to the original concept or requests to redo a substantial portion of the illustration may require a
separate fee, which will be discussed with the client before proceeding.

## 6. REFUNDS AND CANCELLATIONS

Refund requests are considered individually, taking into account the work already completed, the circumstances of the
cancellation, and applicable law.

If the client cancels after payment, the refund may be calculated by deducting the value of the work already completed.

As a general guideline, the commission price may be allocated as follows:

- · Sketch and composition: **25%**.
- · Line art: **25%**.
- · Coloring and final rendering: **50%**.

These percentages are estimates for calculating refunds, not an automatic guarantee of a particular refund amount. If a
stage is only partially completed, its value will be assessed based on the work actually done.

I reserve the right to decline a refund for work already completed, except where a refund is required by applicable law.

If I am unable to complete a commission due to personal circumstances or force majeure, I will refund the portion of the
payment corresponding to work that has not been completed. If I have not started the commission, a full refund will be
issued.

All refund matters will be handled individually. Nothing in these terms limits any consumer rights that cannot legally
be waived.

If the commission is eventually cancelled due to prolonged inactivity, any potential refund will take into account the
work completed, the circumstances, and applicable law.
