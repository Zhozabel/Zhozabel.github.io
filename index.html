Artist · Storyteller · Maker

Zhozabel

My name is Julia, but most people online know me as Zhozabel. I draw comics and illustrations as a hobby, just for fun and love for the process.

I also sometimes write stories, build little worlds of my own, and spend far too much time developing my original characters.

This website is a place to bring all of these interests together — a little home for my comics, art, stories, characters, and whatever else I feel like creating.
